- strategic: long-term direction, goals, priorities, market/product positioning
- operational: process changes, team structure, workflow modifications
- technical: architecture choices, tool selections, implementation approaches
- financial: budget, spend, pricing, cost decisions

- 1.0: clearly and explicitly stated ("We decided to...", "We agreed to...")
- 0.8: strongly implied from context
- 0.6: inferred but uncertain
- Below 0.6: skip — do not include

    {
